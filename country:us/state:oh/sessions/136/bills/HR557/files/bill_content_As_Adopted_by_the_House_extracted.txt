hr557_01_AH As Adopted by the House 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 557 2025-2026 Representative Santucci A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Niles McKinley High School marching band on its Centennial. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate the Niles McKinley High School marching band on the auspicious occasion of its One Hundredth Anniversary; and WHEREAS, Throughout long hours of practice and preparation, the members of the Niles McKinley High School marching band have displayed exceptional musical ability, remarkable confidence, and tremendous discipline. Throughout the past century, the group has always been comprised of some of the most talented young people in the community, and all of the members over the years have proven to be fine musicians; and WHEREAS, Under the capable guidance of its directors, both past and present, the Niles McKinley High School marching band has achieved the balance, blend, and harmony that are the hallmarks of superior performance. These fine leaders have not only encouraged the students to develop their talents to the fullest but have also instilled within them an appreciation of music that will benefit them throughout their lives; and WHEREAS, Participation in a high school band provides a unique opportunity for students to develop their musical skills and to enjoy a special camaraderie while learning the true value of perseverance and cooperation. Over the years, the members of the Niles McKinley High School marching band have excelled in their endeavors, and all those who have been involved with the program are to be commended for their exemplary performances; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, applaud the Niles McKinley High School marching band on its One Hundredth Anniversary and extend best wishes for the future; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Niles McKinley High School marching band.
