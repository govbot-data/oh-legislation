A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring Fierbaugh Farm on its Bicentennial.

WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to pay tribute to Fierbaugh Farm on the celebration of its Two Hundredth Anniversary, October 17, 2026; and

WHEREAS, Recognition of this milestone is, indeed, a fitting tribute to Fierbaugh Farm, for throughout the past two centuries, it has made many invaluable contributions to the surrounding area. Since its establishment in 1826, this family-owned agricultural operation has enjoyed great success in raising sheep and hosting seasonal events, and this anniversary is a justifiable source of pride and an excellent reflection on the homestead, on its owners, and on the Bowerston community as a whole; and

WHEREAS, Those who have nurtured, directed, and sustained Fierbaugh Farm merit praise for their remarkable initiative, diligence, and resolve. With a wealth of vision and vitality, they have shown how very much can be accomplished by a group of conscientious, industrious people, and they can be proud that their unflagging commitment to agriculture has made it possible for the farm to flourish; and

WHEREAS, On this milestone, we praise Fierbaugh Farm for enhancing the quality of farming throughout Bowerston. As this esteemed operation maintains its unwavering dedication to service and achievement, it is certain to continue to grow, prosper, and follow in the tradition of excellence that has long been its hallmark, and we look with optimism to its promising future; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, congratulate Fierbaugh Farm on its Two Hundredth Anniversary and salute it as a fine Ohio endeavor; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Fierbaugh Farm.
