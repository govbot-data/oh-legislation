sr463_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 463 2025-2026 Senator Schaffer A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Emma Strickler on placing first at the 2026 National FFA Convention and Expo. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to extend special recognition to Emma Strickler on winning first place in the Extemporaneous Public Speaking Career Development Event at the 2026 National FFA Convention and Expo; and WHEREAS, Emma Strickler is worthy of distinction, for she has displayed her tremendous potential and dedication in meeting her goals. Clearly, the special acknowledgement she has received for her performance in the Extemporaneous Public Speaking Career Development Event at this year's National FFA Convention and Expo reflects her initiative and resolve; and WHEREAS, Throughout her involvement in FFA, Emma Strickler has demonstrated exemplary perseverance, initiative, and ability, and she can be extremely proud that her devotion to her passions has inspired many to pursue their goals with similar dedication. A member of the Amanda-Clearcreek FFA Chapter, she has proven herself to be a mature young individual of exceptional intelligence and determination, and she has earned the respect and admiration of her friends, family, and community; and WHEREAS, FFA plays a valuable role in the total education of young people, for it is through such worthwhile programs that individuals are taught the value of hard work and afforded an opportunity to develop good leadership skills. Emma Strickler has exemplified the true meaning of citizenship while upholding the highest ideals of the National FFA organization, and she has shown a sincere commitment to bettering the world around her; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend Emma Strickler on her national accolade and offer best wishes for ongoing success; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Emma Strickler.
