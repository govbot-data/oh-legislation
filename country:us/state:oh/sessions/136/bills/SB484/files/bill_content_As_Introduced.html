Section 3. On and after the effective date of this section, the full-time judge of the Struthers Municipal Court, who prior to the effective date of this section was the part-time judge of that court, shall perform the duties of a full-time judge of a municipal court and is subject to any restriction specified in law for a full-time judge of a municipal court.
