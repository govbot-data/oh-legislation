sr459_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 459 2025-2026 Senator DeMora A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team as the 2026 Ohio High School Girls Water Polo State Champion. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to pay tribute to the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team on securing the 2026 Ohio High School Girls Water Polo State Championship; and WHEREAS, The talented Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team is, indeed, to be heartily commended for decisively earning the Ohio High School Girls Water Polo State Championship for a second consecutive year. The squad's performance against Napoleon in its 9-5 win reflects an outstanding collective effort by a gifted group of athletes, and without exception, the team has devoted countless hours to training and practice in order to ensure peak conditioning. Clearly, this demanding regimen has paid handsome dividends; and WHEREAS, The members of the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team have epitomized the power, poise, and precision that befit true champions. Despite sometimes grueling pressure, they have consistently maintained their composure and morale, exhibiting strength, speed, stamina, and agility in every challenging encounter. Time and again, they have risen to the occasion, amply demonstrating their unflagging determination, and their impressive maturity and finesse have earned them widespread respect, even among their most formidable rivals; and WHEREAS, By weathering a rigorous schedule and prevailing over a host of adversaries, the members of the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team have learned much about dedication, self-discipline, leadership, and perseverance. Their first-place finish is a measure of their stalwart commitment to the sport, and their indomitable spirit has made their families, friends, and coaches incredibly proud; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, applaud the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team on its brilliant 2026 season and wish its members continued success in all of their future endeavors; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team.
