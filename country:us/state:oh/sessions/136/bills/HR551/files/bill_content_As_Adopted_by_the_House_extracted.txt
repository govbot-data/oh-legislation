hr551_01_AH As Adopted by the House 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 551 2025-2026 Representative Moore A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Ohio University men’s club hockey team as the 2026 ACHA Division 1 National Champion. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate the Ohio University men's club hockey team on winning the 2026 American Collegiate Hockey Association Division 1 National Championship; and WHEREAS, A remarkable group of young athletes, the members of the Ohio University men's club hockey team have combined talent and spirit with the highest ideals of good sportsmanship to establish themselves as truly exceptional competitors. With admirable dedication, the Bobcats have worked diligently toward attaining ultimate proficiency in hockey, and as a result, they defeated a tough Adrian College team by the score of 3-1 to win this year's ACHA Division 1 National Championship, their first such title since 2004, and finished the season with an impressive 32-6 record; and WHEREAS, The Bobcats' achievements and leadership are a justifiable source of pride and an excellent reflection on the players, their families, their school, the Athens community, and their coaches. In leading their team to victory, Coach Barry Schutte and his assistants have not only helped the athletes to develop their talents to the fullest but have also instilled in them a valuable sense of determination, perseverance, and camaraderie; and WHEREAS, With athletic competition playing such an important role in the total education of our young people, we are extremely pleased to pay tribute to these exemplary athletes and their outstanding coaches for a job well done. The maturity and experience the team members have gained through their participation in sports will greatly aid them in meeting the challenges of the future; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend the Ohio University men's club hockey team on its tremendous season and salute the members and coaches as some of Ohio's finest citizens; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Ohio University men's club hockey team.
