hr533_00_IN As Offered 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 533 2025-2026 Representatives Thomas, D., Santucci A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Nick Strimbu, Inc. on its Centennial. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate Nick Strimbu, Inc., on the auspicious occasion of its One Hundredth Anniversary; and WHEREAS, Recognition of this prestigious milestone is a fitting tribute to Nick Strimbu, for throughout the past one hundred years, it has attained a remarkable record of achievement. Founded in 1926, this family‑owned company has become known for its outstanding trucking services, and it has continually worked to best serve its customers; and WHEREAS, The success of Nick Strimbu is a justifiable source of pride and an outstanding reflection not only on the business itself but also on its astute management and hard-working employees. Due to their vision and initiative, this respected institution has earned the gratitude and appreciation of many satisfied customers, and it is truly deserving of high praise; and WHEREAS, Over the years, Nick Strimbu has enhanced the quality of life within the surrounding area. We are certain that as this noteworthy enterprise maintains its unfaltering dedication to service and achievement, it will continue to prosper and will uphold the tradition of excellence that has long been its hallmark; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend Nick Strimbu, Inc., on its One Hundredth Anniversary and salute it as one of Ohio's finest businesses; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Nick Strimbu, Inc.
