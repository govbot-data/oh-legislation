hr556_01_AH As Adopted by the House 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 556 2025-2026 Representative Hoops A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Grady Short as the 2026 Division II State Boys Golf Champion. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to extend special recognition to Grady Short on winning the 2026 Division II State Championship in boys golf; and WHEREAS, This accomplishment is truly noteworthy, for Grady Short has clearly demonstrated his talent, perseverance, and mental and physical fortitude as a golfer. As this year's Division II State Champion with a two-day total of 141, he has exemplified all of the characteristics that are essential for success both in and out of sports, including courage, determination, and an innate sense of the principles of good sportsmanship; and WHEREAS, In displaying such exceptional prowess as a member of the Archbold High School boys golf team, Grady Short has earned the appreciation of his fellow athletes, the devotion of countless fans, and the respect of even his most determined rivals. Competition in golf has enabled him to learn much about dedication and self‑discipline, and we take this opportunity to acknowledge his exceptional record of achievement; and WHEREAS, In an education system that seeks to teach tomorrow's leaders the fundamentals of self‑reliance and the rewards of individual effort, high school athletics have become a valuable tool. We are proud to salute Grady Short's athletic achievement, and know the skills he has demonstrated here will aid him against all challenges he will face; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, congratulate Grady Short as the 2026 Division II State Boys Golf Champion and look with optimism to a future of success; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Grady Short.
