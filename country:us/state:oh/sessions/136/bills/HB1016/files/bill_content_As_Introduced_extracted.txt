hb1016_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session	H. B. No. 1016 2025-2026 Representative Manning To enact section 5534.623 of the Revised Code to designate a portion of State Route 57 in Lorain County as the "Army Sgt. Daniel Michael Shepherd Memorial Highway." BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF OHIO: Section 1. That section 5534.623 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 5534.623. In addition to any other name prescribed in the Revised Code or otherwise, the northbound and southbound lanes of state route number fifty-seven between chestnut ridge road and west river road, in Lorain county, shall be known as the "Army Sgt. Daniel Michael Shepherd Memorial Highway." The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
