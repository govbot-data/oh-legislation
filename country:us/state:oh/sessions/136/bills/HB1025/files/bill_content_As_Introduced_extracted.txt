hb1025_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session	H. B. No. 1025 2025-2026 Representative Newman Cosponsors: Representatives Pizzulli, Ritter, Claggett, Miller, K., Mathews, T., Creech, Gross, Odioso, Bird, Barhorst, Willis, Click, Klopfenstein, Brennan, Holmes, John, King, Salvo To enact section 5.023 of the Revised Code to designate the Long Rifle of Early Ohio as the official state firearm. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF OHIO: Section 1. That section 5.023 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 5.023. The long rifle of early Ohio is hereby adopted as the official firearm of the state.
