sb473_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session	S. B. No. 473 2025-2026 Senator Antonio Cosponsors: Senators Romanchuk, Brenner, Weinstein, Patton, Smith, DeMora, Timken, Ingram, Lang To enact section 5.64 of the Revised Code to designate September 25 as "Dolly Parton Day." BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF OHIO: Section 1. That section 5.64 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 5.64. The twenty-fifth day of September is designated as "Dolly Parton Day" in recognition of Dolly Parton, the entertainer and philanthropist.
