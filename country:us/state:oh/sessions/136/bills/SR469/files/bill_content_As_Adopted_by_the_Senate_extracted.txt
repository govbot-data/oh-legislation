sr469_01_AS As Adopted by the Senate 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 469 2025-2026 Senator Koehler A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Josie Jennings as the 2026-2027 National FFA President. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to applaud Josie Jennings on being elected the 2026-2027 National FFA President at the 99th National FFA Convention and Expo, October 24, 2026; and WHEREAS, A member of the Global Impact FFA chapter, Josie Jennings has directed herself toward meaningful participation in the many beneficial programs of the National FFA Organization, and she has been a steadfast supporter of this fine group. Her remarkable initiative and dedication have helped to guarantee a high level of success for all projects with which she has been associated, and she has earned the admiration and respect of everyone who knows her; and WHEREAS, A person of intelligence and integrity, Josie Jennings has established high personal goals and has striven to reach her potential, and she has demonstrated an exceptional willingness to work hard to attain superior results. We are certain that as she assumes her duties as the 2026-2027 National FFA President, she will accept each new challenge as an opportunity for growth and achievement; and WHEREAS, FFA plays a valuable role in the total education of young people, for it is through such worthwhile programs that the value of hard work is taught and an opportunity is afforded to develop strong leadership skills. Josie Jennings' unfaltering devotion to FFA has helped her to explore her interests and gain new skills, and we commend her on her numerous accomplishments; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, pay tribute to Josie Jennings on her recent accomplishment and look with optimism to her future endeavors as she commences her term as president; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Josie Jennings.
