hr585_00_IN As Offered 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 585 2025-2026 Representative Synenberg A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Beachwood High School girls 4x200-meter relay team as a 2026 State Champion in the Division III Indoor and the Division IV Outdoor state meets. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to commend the Beachwood High School girls 4x200-meter relay team on its first-place finish at the 2026 Division III Indoor State and Division IV Outdoor State championships; and WHEREAS, Establishing a reputation for skill and tenacity, the Beachwood High School girls' 4x200-meter relay team has emerged as a powerhouse in high school track and field. This year, the squad claimed state titles at the 2026 Division III Indoor State Championship and the Division IV Outdoor State Championship. These historic victories have cemented the team's legacy and will be celebrated for generations of Beachwood athletes to come; and WHEREAS, The Beachwood High School girls' 4x200-meter relay team rose to the occasion and proved itself a dynamic force in high school track and field. This exceptional group of athletes can take pride in the hard-earned accomplishments and well-deserved success it achieved throughout the 2026 season; and WHEREAS, Embodying the motivation, poise, and determination of true champions, the members of the Beachwood High School girls' 4x200-meter relay team, under the guidance of Coach Willie Smith, positioned themselves to claim this year's state crowns. Throughout the season, the Bison consistently demonstrated exceptional energy, enthusiasm, and dedication, qualities that helped propel them to championship success; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, congratulate the Beachwood High School girls 4x200-meter relay team on its success and praise the athletes and coach for their tremendous prowess; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Beachwood High School girls 4x200-meter relay team.
