hr569_00_IN As Offered 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 569 2025-2026 Representative Peterson A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Brewer Tomlinson as a 2026 Division V State Champion. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to applaud Brewer Tomlinson on winning first place in the shot put at the 2026 Division V State Track and Field Championship; and WHEREAS, Brewer Tomlinson has proven to be a talented and superbly conditioned athlete who has earned the admiration of fans and opponents alike for his spirited and highly motivated performances. Throughout the season, he has worked diligently to improve his skills, and as a result, he accomplished his goals and earned the state championship with a throw of 59 feet, 7.5 inches; and WHEREAS, Known as a driven and determined individual, Brewer Tomlinson has developed into a remarkably dedicated and efficient athlete whose skill is equaled only by his exemplary display of sportsmanship. Indeed, he is a credit not only to his family and Eastern High School but also to the superb leadership of his coaches; and WHEREAS, The road to victory is a difficult one, requiring ability, endurance, and a positive attitude on the part of athletes and coaches alike. In all of his sports endeavors, Brewer Tomlinson has overcome a variety of challenges, and his tremendous efforts are worthy of emulation; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, salute Brewer Tomlinson on his brilliant season and wish him the best in the years to come; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Brewer Tomlinson.
