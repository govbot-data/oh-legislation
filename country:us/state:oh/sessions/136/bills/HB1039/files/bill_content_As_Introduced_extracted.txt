hb1039_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session	H. B. No. 1039 2025-2026 Representative Piccolantonio Cosponsors: Representatives Brennan, Lett, Rogers To enact section 5534.458 of the Revised Code to designate the bridge connecting I-270 southbound to I-70 eastbound as the "Specialist Nicolas E. Messmer Memorial Bridge." BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF OHIO: Section 1. That section 5534.458 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 5534.458. The bridge connecting the southbound lanes of interstate route number two hundred seventy to the eastbound lanes of interstate route number seventy, located in Franklin county, shall be known as the "Specialist Nicolas E. Messmer Memorial Bridge." The director of transportation may erect suitable markers upon the bridge or its approaches indicating its name.
