sb483_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session	S. B. No. 483 2025-2026 Senator Koehler Cosponsors: Senators Brenner, Schaffer, Smith To enact section 5534.157 of the Revised Code to designate a portion of United States Route 68 in Clinton County as the "Captain Curtis Angst Memorial Highway." BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF OHIO: Section 1. That section 5534.157 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 5534.157. In addition to any other name prescribed in the Revised Code or otherwise, the northbound and southbound lanes of United States route number sixty-eight between interstate route number seventy-one and the northern boundary of the municipal corporation of Wilmington, in Clinton county, shall be known as the "Captain Curtis Angst Memorial Highway." The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
