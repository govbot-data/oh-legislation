sr467_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 467 2025-2026 Senator McColley A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Liberty Center High School girls cross country team as the 2026 Division IV State Champion. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to salute the Liberty Center High School girls cross country team on winning the 2026 Division IV State Championship; and WHEREAS, Establishing a reputation for skill and tenacity, the Liberty Center High School girls cross country team is an impressive group of athletes whose talent and teamwork are unmatched. At the 2026 Division IV State Championship, the squad amassed 112 points and seized the title, its fourth state championship in program history, and this victory will be remembered for many years to come; and WHEREAS, The members of the Liberty Center High School girls cross country team, including Colbie Strayer, Castyn Miller, Mya Stark, Ryley Miller, Brynn Gyurasics, Emma Bailey, and Hailey Bowen, rose to the occasion and proved themselves to be a dynamic force in high school sports. Truly, these outstanding athletes can be proud of the accomplishments that were hard-won and deserved throughout the season; and WHEREAS, By remaining faithful to the highest standards of sportsmanship and teamwork, the members of the Liberty Center High School girls cross country team primed themselves under the guidance of Coach Ken Barnes to reach their full potential. Indeed, the Tigers have demonstrated remarkable energy, enthusiasm, and dedication in all of their endeavors; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, recognize the Liberty Center High School girls cross country team on its success and commend the athletes and coach for their tremendous prowess; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Liberty Center High School girls cross country team.
