Representative Synenberg


A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring the Beachwood High School Girls Track and Field Team on winning two State championships.

WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate the Beachwood High School Girls Track and Field Team on winning the 2026 Ohio Association of Track and Cross Country Coaches Indoor Division III and the Ohio High School Athletic Association Division IV state championships; and

WHEREAS, A remarkable group of young athletes, the members of the Beachwood High School Girls Track and Field Team have combined talent and spirit with the highest ideals of good sportsmanship to establish themselves as truly exceptional competitors. With admirable dedication, the Bison have worked diligently toward attaining ultimate proficiency in their track and field events, and as a result, they won the 2026 OATCCC Division III Indoor Championship and went on to score 53 points and secure the OHSAA Division IV State Championship; and

WHEREAS, The achievements and leadership of the Beachwood High School Girls Track and Field Team are a justifiable source of pride and an excellent reflection on the athletes, their families, and their school community. In leading the team to victory, the coaching staff has helped these athletes to develop great talent by instilling the valuable lessons of determination, perseverance, and camaraderie; and

WHEREAS, With athletic competition playing such an important role in the total education of our young people, we are pleased to pay tribute to the exemplary athletes and the outstanding coaches of the Beachwood High School Girls Track and Field Team for a job well done. The maturity and experience the team members have gained through their participation in sports will greatly aid them in meeting the challenges of the future; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend the Beachwood High School Girls Track and Field Team on its tremendous 2026 performance and salute the members and coaches as some of Ohio's finest citizens; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Beachwood High School Girls Track and Field Team.
