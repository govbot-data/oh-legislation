sr430_01_AS As Adopted by the Senate 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 430 2025-2026 Senator Romanchuk A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Carton Service CSI, LLC on its One Hundredth Anniversary. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to commend Carton Service CSI, LLC, on the auspicious occasion of its One Hundredth Anniversary; and WHEREAS, Recognition of this prestigious milestone is, indeed, a fitting tribute to Carton Service, for over the past one hundred years, this fine business has attained a remarkable record of service by producing a variety of folding and gable-top cartons made of sustainable paperboard. Since its founding in 1926, the company has continually demonstrated its exemplary commitment to the highest industry standards, customer satisfaction, and competitive pricing, and its devotion to the best interests of its clientele has been evident in all its transactions; and WHEREAS, The success of Carton Service is a justifiable source of pride and an outstanding reflection not only on the business itself but also on its astute management and hard-working employees. Their vision and initiative have earned this respected institution the gratitude and esteem of many appreciative customers, and they are truly deserving of high praise; and WHEREAS, Over the years, Carton Service has enhanced the quality of life within the surrounding community and has contributed greatly to the area's economic well-being. We are certain that as this worthy enterprise maintains its dedication to service and achievement, it will continue to prosper and will uphold the tradition of excellence that has long been its hallmark; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, congratulate Carton Service CSI, LLC, on its Centennial and extend best wishes for the future; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Carton Service CSI, LLC.
