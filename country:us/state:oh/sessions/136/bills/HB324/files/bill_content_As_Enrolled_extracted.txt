hb324_05_EN (136th General Assembly) (Substitute House Bill Number 324) AN ACT To enact section 3715.39 of the Revised Code to establish conditions on the prescribing of drugs subject to a federal risk evaluation and mitigation strategy and to name this act the Patient Protection Act. Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Ohio: Section 1. That section 3715.39 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 3715.39. (A) As used in this section, "prescriber" has the same meaning as in section 4729.01 of the Revised Code, except that a prescriber does not include a veterinarian licensed under Chapter 4741. of the Revised Code. (B) Before a prescriber may issue a prescription for a drug for which the United States food and drug administration requires a risk evaluation and mitigation strategy under section 355-1 of the "Federal Food, Drug, and Cosmetic Act," 21 U.S.C. 355-1, the prescriber shall do all of the following: (1) Conduct an in-person examination of the patient; (2) Inform the patient that the drug is subject to a risk evaluation and mitigation strategy, which may include guidelines or requirements for the patient's use of the drug; (3) Schedule the patient for a follow-up appointment. Section 2. This act shall be known as the Patient Protection Act. Speaker ___________________ of the House of Representatives. President ___________________ of the Senate.
