A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring Chris Mabe on his service to the OPERS Board of Trustees.

WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to pay tribute to Chris Mabe on his retirement from the Ohio Public Employees Retirement System Board of Trustees; and

WHEREAS, Diligent and painstaking in all his endeavors, Chris Mabe has achieved a noteworthy record of service to the Ohio Public Employees Retirement System Board of Trustees, where he has served as both vice chairman and chairman. Indeed, he has brought to his position a wealth of experience gained as a Sergeant with the U.S. Army, a sergeant at the Lorain Correctional Institution, the vice president and president of OCSEA, the international vice president of AFSCME International, and a member of the Ohio AFL-CIO Executive Board, and he has shown a sincere commitment to serving OPERS in an efficient and professional manner; and

WHEREAS, An individual of exceptional civic concern and commitment, Chris Mabe has worked to advance the goals of the agency in a painstaking manner, never failing to overcome even the most seemingly insurmountable obstacle. By approaching difficult decisions and shouldering responsibilities with confidence, he has gained the admiration of everyone who appreciates integrity, intelligence, and determination; and

WHEREAS, To his credit, Chris Mabe has demonstrated how very much a conscientious, industrious person can accomplish, and he can be proud that his unflagging dedication to the Ohio Public Employees Retirement System has inspired many who know him to devote themselves similarly. Clearly, his contributions will not be forgotten, and he is deserving of high praise; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend Chris Mabe on his exemplary service and wish him happiness and fulfillment in the years to come; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Chris Mabe.
