A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring Brittney Nolletti as a 2026 Adidas Middle School Outdoor National Champion.

WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to pay tribute to Brittney Nolletti on placing first at the 2026 Adidas Middle School Outdoor National Championship in the girls pole vault; and

WHEREAS, Brittney Nolletti's outstanding ability and spirited drive, as well as her quickness and tenacity, have distinguished her as a superior athlete and an exemplary student at Triway Middle School. After a tremendous performance at the 2026 OHSAA 7th and 8th Grade State Track Championship meet, where she set a new record with a vault of 11 feet, 9 inches, she exceeded all expectations by placing first in the girls pole vault with a personal best mark of 10 feet, 11.1 inches at this year's Adidas Middle School Outdoor National Championship, and she has gained the respect and admiration of all those who have witnessed her in action; and

WHEREAS, Few sports embody the spirit of individual accomplishment more than the pole vault. Athletes such as Brittney Nolletti exemplify the qualities necessary for excellence in any sport, including discipline, agility, conditioning, and focus. Indeed, throughout long hours of practice and competition, she has consistently demonstrated the talent and motivation that are the hallmarks of a true competitor; and

WHEREAS, Through her participation in sports, Brittney Nolletti has learned lessons of perseverance, hard work, and fair and honest competition that will undoubtedly be of great benefit to her throughout her lifetime. Time and again, she has risen to the occasion, maintaining her morale, composure, and good sportsmanship, and she has set a fine example that is sure to inspire future generations; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, congratulate Brittney Nolletti on her outstanding performance at the 2026 Adidas Middle School Outdoor National Championship in the girls pole vault and offer best wishes for success in the future; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Brittney Nolletti.
