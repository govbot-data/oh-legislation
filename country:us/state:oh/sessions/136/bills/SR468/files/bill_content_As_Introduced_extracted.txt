sr468_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 468 2025-2026 Senator Koehler A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Wilmington FFA Chapter as the 2026 National Model of Excellence. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate the Wilmington FFA Chapter on being named the 2026 National Model of Excellence at the 99th National FFA Convention & Expo; and WHEREAS, All those associated with the Wilmington FFA Chapter have truly distinguished themselves as remarkable individuals. Among their activities this year, they hosted students from FFA chapters across Ohio in a mock job application and interview process and prepared and delivered hot meals to area farmers during the harvest season. Indeed, the members of this fine organization have displayed a degree of determination and motivation of which they can be proud, and they have set an example of excellence that is worthy of emulation; and WHEREAS, The members of the Wilmington FFA Chapter warrant the highest praise for their exceptional diligence and resolve, as well as for their unwavering dedication to the goals and principles of the National FFA Organization. It is certainly encouraging to know that the future of Ohio agriculture and agribusiness lies, to a large extent, in the capable hands of conscientious individuals such as these; and WHEREAS, FFA combines the wisdom and leadership of adulthood with the enthusiasm and optimism of youth so that our students will be better prepared to assume meaningful, rewarding roles in society. The maturity, self-confidence, and knowledge that the members of the Wilmington FFA Chapter have gained through their participation in the program will no doubt be of great benefit to them throughout their lifetimes, and it is not surprising that their efforts have won them a host of admirers; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, salute the Wilmington FFA Chapter for outstanding achievement and extend best wishes for the future; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Wilmington FFA Chapter.
