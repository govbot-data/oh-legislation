sr470_01_AS As Adopted by the Senate 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 470 2025-2026 Senator Craig Cosponsors: Senators Antonio, Blackshear, Blessing, Brenner, Chavez, Cirino, Cutrona, DeMora, Gavarone, Hicks-Hudson, Huffman, Ingram, Johnson, Koehler, Landis, Lang, Liston, Manchester, McColley, O'Brien, Patton, Reineke, Reynolds, Roegner, Romanchuk, Schaffer, Smith, Timken, Weinstein, Wilkin, Wilson A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Robin Thompson on her retirement from the Ohio Legislative Service Commission. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate Robin Thompson on retiring from the Ohio Legislative Service Commission; and WHEREAS, Robin Thompson is a truly remarkable individual, for she has combined civic concern and diligence with the utmost professionalism to become a dynamic leader in her field. Joining the Ohio Legislative Service Commission more than twenty-six years ago, she has assiduously served as a bill preparation specialist, and she has performed her duties and fulfilled her responsibilities with the utmost efficiency and competence; and WHEREAS, Throughout her career with the Ohio Legislative Service Commission, Robin Thompson has given generously of her time, energy, and abilities to assist the General Assembly, and she has demonstrated a noteworthy dedication to the success of state government. Always willing to go above and beyond expectations, she has provided a valuable service by helping to maintain the efficiency and effectiveness of the legislative process, and she is deserving of high praise; and WHEREAS, We are proud to note that it is through the unsurpassed efforts and deep commitment of conscientious people such as Robin Thompson that the State of Ohio continues to prosper. Over the years, she has striven to serve her fellow citizens to the best of her ability, and we applaud her on her fine work; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend Robin Thompson on the occasion of her retirement and wish her all the best in the years to come; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Robin Thompson.
