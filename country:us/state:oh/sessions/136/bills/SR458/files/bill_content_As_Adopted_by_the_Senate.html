Senator Brenner


A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring the River View High School boys golf team on securing the 2026 Division II State Championship.

WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to applaud the River View High School boys golf team as the 2026 Division II State Champion; and

WHEREAS, The River View High School boys golf team is made up of remarkable athletes who have worked diligently to showcase the collective talents of their exceptional squad. At this year's Division II State Tournament, the Black Bears won the state crown with a total score of 600, and the members amply demonstrated impressive determination, maturity, and finesse in pursuit of this achievement; and

WHEREAS, This triumph reflects the staunch resolve of these young individuals, and it signifies the acquisition of valuable experience that is sure to benefit them in later life. Thanks to the tireless efforts of an exceptional River View High School boys golf team coaching staff, including their head coach, Chad Gress, as well as the support of their Black Bear community, these athletes have met the demands of an extensive training regimen, and they have just cause for celebration; and

WHEREAS, All those who comprise the River View High School boys golf team have set an excellent example of what a dedicated group of athletes can achieve when guided by a committed coaching staff and surrounded by loyal fans, sports families, and boosters. These young competitors have certainly proven themselves to be dynamic athletic leaders, and we are confident that as they meet future challenges, they will take with them the valuable lessons they have gained through their participation in golf; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, congratulate the River View High School boys golf team on its 2026 Division II State Championship title and count its members and coaches among the ranks of outstanding Ohioans; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the River View High School boys golf team.
