sb486_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session	S. B. No. 486 2025-2026 Senator Schaffer To enact section 5534.016 of the Revised Code to designate a portion of State Route 674 in Fairfield County and Pickaway County as the "Captain Seth R. Koval Memorial Highway." BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF OHIO: Section 1. That section 5534.016 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 5534.016. In addition to any other name prescribed in the Revised Code or otherwise, that portion of state route number six hundred seventy-four, running in a northerly and southerly direction, between the intersection of that route and United States route number twenty-two and the intersection of that route and state route number seven hundred fifty-two, within Fairfield county and Pickaway county, shall be known as the "Captain Seth R. Koval Memorial Highway." The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
