hr518_01_AH As Adopted by the House 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 518 2025-2026 Representative Mathews, T. A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Sink’s Flower Shop on its Centennial. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate Sink's Flower Shop on the auspicious occasion of its One Hundredth Anniversary, celebrated with a ribbon cutting, July 30, 2026; and WHEREAS, Recognition of this prestigious milestone is a fitting tribute to Sink's Flower Shop, for throughout the past century, this fine business has attained a remarkable record of service to the Findlay and Hancock County area. Since 1926, this premier florist has offered the freshest flowers and most beautiful arrangements for every occasion, and its dedication to the best interests of its clientele has been evident in all its transactions; and WHEREAS, The success of Sink's Flower Shop is a justifiable source of pride and an outstanding reflection not only on the venture itself but also on its astute management and on its hard-working employees. This respected enterprise has earned the gratitude and appreciation of many satisfied customers due to the vision and initiative of its staff, and it is truly deserving of high praise; and WHEREAS, Over the years, Sink's Flower Shop has enhanced the quality of life within the surrounding area. We are certain that as this worthy establishment maintains its unwavering dedication to service and achievement, it will continue to prosper and will uphold the tradition of excellence that has long been its hallmark; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend Sink's Flower Shop on its One Hundredth Anniversary and extend best wishes for the future; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Sink's Flower Shop.
