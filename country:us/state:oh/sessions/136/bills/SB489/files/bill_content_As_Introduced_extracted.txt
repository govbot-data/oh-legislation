sb489_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session	S. B. No. 489 2025-2026 Senator Craig Cosponsors: Senators Antonio, Brenner, DeMora, Smith To enact section 5.2325 of the Revised Code to designate January as "Ohio Mentoring Month." BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF OHIO: Section 1. That section 5.2325 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 5.2325. The month of January is designated as "Ohio Mentoring Month" to emphasize the importance of mentorship in supporting personal growth, educational achievement, and professional development, and to acknowledge those who dedicate their time and experience to guiding and supporting others.
