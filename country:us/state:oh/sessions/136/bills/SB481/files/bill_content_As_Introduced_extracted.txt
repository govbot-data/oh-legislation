sb481_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session	S. B. No. 481 2025-2026 Senator Craig Cosponsors: Senators Antonio, Smith, DeMora, Brenner, Schaffer To enact section 5.2325 of the Revised Code to designate October 8 as "Captain Eddie Rickenbacker Day." BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF OHIO: Section 1. That section 5.2325 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 5.2325. The eighth day of October is designated as "Captain Eddie Rickenbacker Day" to honor the life and legacy of Columbus-born Captain Edward Vernon Rickenbacker and his contributions to military service, aviation, automobile racing, transportation, and the history of this state and nation.
