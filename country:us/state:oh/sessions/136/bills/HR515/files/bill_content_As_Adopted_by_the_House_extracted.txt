hr515_01_AH As Adopted by the House 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 515 2025-2026 Representative Deeter A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Cohen Reer as a 2026 Division III State Wrestling Champion. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate Cohen Reer on winning the 2026 Division III State Wrestling Championship in the 106-pound weight class; and WHEREAS, A student at Edison High School, Cohen Reer has combined talent and competitive spirit with the highest ideals of good sportsmanship to establish himself as a truly exceptional young athlete. With admirable dedication, he has worked diligently toward attaining ultimate proficiency in wrestling, and as a result, he defeated a tough opponent from Eastwood High School by the score of 19-4 to win this year's Division III State Championship in the 106-pound weight class; and WHEREAS, Cohen Reer's accomplishments and leadership, both in and out of competition, are a justifiable source of pride and a fine reflection not only on him but also on his family, his teammates, his coaches, his school, and his community. His unwavering commitment to excellence has earned him the respect and esteem of many, and he is certainly worthy of high praise; and WHEREAS, With athletic competition playing such an important role in the total education of our young people, we are overjoyed to pay tribute to Cohen Reer on an exemplary season. Indeed, the same maturity and experience he has gained through his participation in wrestling will greatly aid him in meeting the challenges of the future; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend Cohen Reer as a 2026 Division III State Wrestling Champion and extend best wishes for continued success; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Cohen Reer.
