A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring Deer Park Community City Schools on its Bicentennial.

WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate Deer Park Community City Schools on the auspicious occasion of its Two Hundredth Anniversary, celebrated October 10, 2026; and

WHEREAS, Recognition of this prestigious milestone is, indeed, a fitting tribute to Deer Park Community City Schools, for throughout the past two centuries, it has played a valuable role in preparing countless young people to meet the challenges of the future. With an unwavering commitment to educational excellence, the district has worked diligently to offer students a well‑balanced curriculum that seeks to develop their academic and practical skills, and it has gained the gratitude and esteem of pupils, parents, and the community alike; and

WHEREAS, Schools provide scholars with much more than the basic educational skills, and we are certain that in the years to come, Deer Park Community City Schools will maintain its unfaltering dedication to helping students become capable, well-rounded individuals. As the district celebrates this special anniversary, its students, staff, and alumni can be justly proud of this fine institution; and

WHEREAS, Education is vital to our nation's continued economic and social well-being, and as the need for global learning and understanding increases, so do the demands placed upon our system of education. Over the years, the administrators and teachers of Deer Park Community City Schools have consistently and effectively met these challenges, and all those associated with the district are worthy of applause; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend Deer Park Community City Schools on its Two Hundredth Anniversary and extend best wishes for ongoing success; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Deer Park Community City Schools.
