sr462_01_AS As Adopted by the Senate 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 462 2025-2026 Senator Antonio A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the City of Westlake on its Two Hundred Fifteenth Anniversary. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to pay tribute to the City of Westlake on the auspicious occasion of its Two Hundred Fifteenth Anniversary, celebrated November 14, 2026; and WHEREAS, This is a momentous occasion, and it provides an ideal time to acknowledge the exceptional achievements of the City of Westlake. This fine municipality can be proud of its history, which reflects impressive development over the generations, and it has become an important part of the Northeast Ohio community; and WHEREAS, Established in 1811, the City of Westlake has enjoyed the benefits of a generous and civic-minded population, and those who currently live and work in the area can take great pride in their rich heritage. Their noteworthy initiative and vision have had a profound impact, and we applaud Westlake's residents for their commitment to the continued well-being of their respective communities. They have, indeed, set an example of concerned and responsible citizenship worthy of emulation; and WHEREAS, Every state's survival and stability depends upon vibrant and durable communities, and two hundred fifteen years after the City of Westlake was founded, it still radiates vitality, optimism, and a perpetual charm all its own. This is certainly a place of many engaging qualities, and its schools, businesses, and civic organizations have embodied and upheld the values that are fundamental to Ohio's success; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend the City of Westlake on the commemoration of its Two Hundred Fifteenth Anniversary and offer our best wishes for a prosperous future; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the City of Westlake.
