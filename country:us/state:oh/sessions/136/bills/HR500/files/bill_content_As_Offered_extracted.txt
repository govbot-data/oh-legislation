WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th 3
General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate the Village of 4
Meyers Lake on the Centennial of its incorporation; and 5
WHEREAS, Founded in 1812, Meyers Lake was incorporated as a 6
village in 1927, and recognition of the One Hundredth Anniversary of 7
this event is, indeed, a fitting tribute to this fine community. Over 8
more than two centuries, it has become known for its residents' 9
cooperation, integrity, and willingness to work hard, exemplary 10
qualities that are still manifested by its citizens today; and 11
WHEREAS, Throughout Meyers Lake's existence, its generous and 12
civic-minded population has enjoyed a wealth of benefits, and those 13
who currently make the village their home take great pride in their 14
community and its rich heritage. Their efforts have certainly made the 15
area a pleasant and friendly place in which to live and work; and 16
WHEREAS, America is only as strong as her individual communities, 17
which are unique and which make significant contributions to the 18
culture of our state and nation. The Centennial of the Village of 19
Meyers Lake offers its citizens the opportunity to share their 20
memories and experiences and to reflect on the benefits of life in 21
their close-knit community, for they have built on the best traditions 22
of the past to ensure a future filled with hope and promise; therefore 23
be it 24
RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of 25
the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, 26
commend the Village of Meyers Lake on the One Hundredth Anniversary of 27
