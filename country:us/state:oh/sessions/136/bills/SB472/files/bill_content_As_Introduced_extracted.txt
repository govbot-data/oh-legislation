sb472_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session	S. B. No. 472 2025-2026 Senator Craig Cosponsors: Senators Ingram, Antonio, Weinstein, DeMora, Blackshear, Hicks-Hudson, Liston, Smith To enact section 5534.506 of the Revised Code to designate a portion of U.S. Route 62 in Columbus as the "State Senator I. Ray Miller Jr. Memorial Highway." BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF OHIO: Section 1. That section 5534.506 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 5534.506. In addition to any other name prescribed in the Revised Code or otherwise, the eastbound and westbound lanes of United States route number sixty-two between interstate route number seventy-one and Monypenny avenue, in the municipal corporation of Columbus, shall be known as the "State Senator I. Ray Miller Jr. Memorial Highway." The director of transportation may erect suitable markers along the highway indicating its name.
