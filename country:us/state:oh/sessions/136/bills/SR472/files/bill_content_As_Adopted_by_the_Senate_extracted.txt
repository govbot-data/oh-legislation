sr472_01_AS As Adopted by the Senate 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 472 2025-2026 Senator Blackshear A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Oakwood High School girls cross country team on winning the 2026 Division III State Championship. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to pay tribute to the Oakwood High School girls cross country team on securing the 2026 Division III State Championship; and WHEREAS, The talented Oakwood High School girls cross country team is, indeed, to be heartily commended for decisively earning this year's Division III State Championship, its second consecutive such title. The squad's performance reflects the outstanding collective effort by a gifted group of athletes, and without exception, the team has devoted countless hours to training and practice in order to ensure peak conditioning. Clearly, this demanding regimen has paid handsome dividends; and WHEREAS, The members of the Oakwood High School girls cross country team have epitomized the power, poise, and precision that befit true champions. Despite sometimes grueling pressure, they have consistently maintained their composure and morale, exhibiting strength, speed, stamina, and agility in every challenging encounter. Time and again, they have risen to the occasion, amply demonstrating their unflagging determination, and their impressive maturity and finesse have earned them widespread respect, even among their most formidable rivals; and WHEREAS, By weathering a rigorous schedule and prevailing over a host of adversaries, the members of the Oakwood High School girls cross country team have learned much about dedication, self-discipline, leadership, and perseverance. Their first-place finish is a measure of their stalwart commitment to the sport, and their indomitable spirit has made their families, friends, and coach, Kurt Sanford, incredibly proud; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, applaud the Oakwood High School girls cross country team on its brilliant 2026 season and wish its members success in all of their future endeavors; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Oakwood High School girls cross country team.
