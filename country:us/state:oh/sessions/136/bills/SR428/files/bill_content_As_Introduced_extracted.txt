sr428_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 428 2025-2026 Senator Schaffer A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Kendall Wild as the 2026 National Miss Amazing Teen Queen. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to pay tribute to Kendall Wild on being crowned the National Miss Amazing Teen Queen for 2026; and WHEREAS, An intelligent, poised, and motivated individual, Kendall Wild has developed her knowledge and skills and has striven to meet challenges with enthusiasm and confidence. A remarkable young woman, she has displayed an exceptional willingness to work hard to advocate for those with visual impairments and blindness, including writing a children's book about a deaf and blind alpaca, volunteering at numerous events, and donating books in braille to her local library as well as winning first place for her project at the FFA Agriscience Fair at the Ohio FFA Convention and volunteering with the Ohio 4-H Teen Leadership Council. For her unfaltering devotion to excellence, she has been selected as this year's National Miss Amazing Teen Queen; and WHEREAS, Kendall Wild has derived extra energy from her many activities and accomplishments, and her integrity and steadfast determination are truly admirable. Indeed, her amiable style will carry her far beyond this event and will be of great value to her in the years to come; and WHEREAS, The achievements of Kendall Wild are a justifiable source of pride not only for her family and friends but also for the entire Canal Winchester community. She has shown a sincere desire to fully develop her talents so that she might fulfill her goals in the future, and this, coupled with her responsibility and initiative, shows her potential to serve as an outstanding ambassador for our state; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend Kendall Wild on her recent accolade and extend best wishes for continued happiness and fulfillment; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Kendall Wild.
