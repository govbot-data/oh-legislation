hr539_00_IN As Offered 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 539 2025-2026 Representative Young A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Star City Paint & Decorating on its One Hundredth Anniversary. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to commend Star City Paint & Decorating on the auspicious occasion of its One Hundredth Anniversary; and WHEREAS, Recognition of this prestigious milestone is, indeed, a fitting tribute to Star City Paint & Decorating, for throughout the past century, this fine business has attained a remarkable record of service to the Greater Dayton area. Since its founding, this family-owned, independently operated paint store has continually demonstrated its exemplary commitment to the highest industry standards and competitive pricing, and its dedication to the best interests of its clientele has been evident in its efforts to remain current for generations of customers; and WHEREAS, The success of Star City Paint & Decorating is a justifiable source of pride and an outstanding reflection not only on the enterprise itself but also on its astute management and its hard-working employees. Their vision and initiative have earned this respected institution the gratitude and appreciation of many satisfied customers, and they are truly deserving of high praise; and WHEREAS, Over the years, Star City Paint & Decorating has enhanced the quality of life within the surrounding community and contributed greatly to the area's economic well-being. We are certain that as this worthy enterprise maintains its dedication to service and achievement, it will continue to prosper and will uphold the tradition of excellence that has long been its hallmark; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, congratulate Star City Paint & Decorating on its Centennial and extend best wishes for the future; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Star City Paint & Decorating.
