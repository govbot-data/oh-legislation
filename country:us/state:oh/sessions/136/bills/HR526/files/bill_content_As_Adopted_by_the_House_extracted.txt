hr526_01_AH As Adopted by the House 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 526 2025-2026 Representatives Salvo, Kishman A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Bradlee Keehn as a 2026 Division IV OHSAA State Track and Field Champion. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to pay tribute to Bradlee Keehn on winning the 2026 Ohio High School Athletic Association Division IV State Championship in the 800-meter run; and WHEREAS, This accomplishment is truly noteworthy, for Bradlee Keehn has clearly demonstrated his talent and perseverance, as well as his mental and physical fortitude as a runner. As this year's Division IV State Champion in the 800-meter run with a time of 1:55.77, he has exemplified all of the characteristics that are essential for success both in and out of the sports world, including courage, determination, and an innate sense of the principles of good sportsmanship; and WHEREAS, In displaying such outstanding prowess as a senior member of the Fairless High School track and field team, Bradlee Keehn has earned the appreciation of his fellow athletes, the devotion of countless fans, and the respect of even his most determined rivals. Competition in athletics has enabled him to learn much about dedication and self-discipline, and we take this opportunity to acknowledge his exemplary record of achievement; and WHEREAS, Running is an event that requires exceptional stamina and natural ability, and Bradlee Keehn is to be commended for his commitment to this demanding activity. In striving to attain his athletic goals, he has consistently exhibited the skill and integrity that are the hallmarks of a true champion; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, applaud Bradlee Keehn as the 2026 OHSAA Division IV State Champion in the 800-meter run and salute him as a fine Ohioan; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Bradlee Keehn.
