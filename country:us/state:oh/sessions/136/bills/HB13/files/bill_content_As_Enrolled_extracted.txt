hb13_05_EN (136th General Assembly) (House Bill Number 13) AN ACT To enact section 5.267 of the Revised Code to designate November 5th as "Roy Rogers Day." Be it enacted by the General Assembly of the State of Ohio: Section 1. That section 5.267 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 5.267. The fifth day of November is designated as "Roy Rogers Day," in honor of Roy Rogers, also known as "King of the Cowboys," who was a singer and actor from Portsmouth, Ohio.
