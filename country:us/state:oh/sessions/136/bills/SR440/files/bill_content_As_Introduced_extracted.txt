sr440_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 440 2025-2026 Senator Landis A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Waynedale High School wrestling team as the 2026 Division III State Dual Champion. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to pay tribute to the Waynedale High School wrestling team on securing the 2026 Division III State Dual Championship; and WHEREAS, The talented Waynedale High School wrestling team is, indeed, to be heartily commended for decisively earning this year's 2026 Division III State Dual Championship, its first such crown in program and Wayne County history. The squad's performance, which included a 38-14 victory over the team from Archbold High School, reflects the outstanding collective effort by a gifted group of athletes, and without exception, the team has devoted countless hours to training and practice in order to ensure peak conditioning. Clearly, this demanding regimen has paid handsome dividends; and WHEREAS, The members of the Waynedale High School wrestling team have epitomized the power, poise, and precision that befit true champions. Despite sometimes grueling pressure, they have consistently maintained their composure and morale, exhibiting strength, speed, stamina, and agility in every challenging encounter. Time and again, they have risen to the occasion, amply demonstrating their unflagging determination, and their impressive maturity and finesse have earned them widespread respect, even among their most formidable rivals; and WHEREAS, By weathering a rigorous schedule and prevailing over a host of adversaries, the members of the Waynedale High School wrestling team have learned much about dedication, self‑discipline, leadership, and perseverance. Their first-place finish is a measure of their stalwart commitment to the sport, and their indomitable spirit has made their families, friends, and coach, Brenden Stanley, incredibly proud; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, applaud the Waynedale High School wrestling team on its brilliant 2026 season and wish its members continued success in all of their future endeavors; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Waynedale High School wrestling team.
