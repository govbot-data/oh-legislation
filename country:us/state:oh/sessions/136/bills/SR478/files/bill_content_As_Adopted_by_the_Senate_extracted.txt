sr478_01_AS As Adopted by the Senate 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 478 2025-2026 Senator DeMora A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Upper Arlington High School field hockey team as the 2026 OHSAA State Champion. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to extend special recognition to the Upper Arlington High School field hockey team on securing the 2026 Ohio High School Athletic Association Championship; and WHEREAS, The Upper Arlington High School field hockey team has achieved a tremendous feat in winning its first-ever state championship crown, and the culmination of an exemplary season was marked by tremendous achievement. The Golden Bears' strong performance, combined with an adept drive to succeed, has led the squad to this outstanding victory, and the group has earned the appreciation and admiration of many; and WHEREAS, The accomplishments and development of the Upper Arlington High School field hockey team are fine examples of what can be attained by a group of hard-working young people supported by a dedicated coaching staff. Throughout extensive conditioning and training under the guidance of their coach, Homero Pardi, the members have become dynamic competitors, and they are deserving of acknowledgement; and WHEREAS, With great admiration, we congratulate the athletes who comprise the Upper Arlington High School field hockey team, for they have overcome many formidable opponents and displayed tremendous prowess. Indeed, we are confident that as they meet their future challenges, they will take with them the lessons of camaraderie, diligence, and discipline; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, heartily applaud the Upper Arlington High School field hockey team as the 2026 OHSAA State Champion and praise the strength of this program and its participating athletes; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Upper Arlington High School field hockey team.
