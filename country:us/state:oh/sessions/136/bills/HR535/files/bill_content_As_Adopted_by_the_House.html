Representative Young


A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring Yariel Soto Torrado as a 2026 Central American and Caribbean Games Champion.

WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to commend Yariel Soto Torrado on securing a gold medal in the decathlon at the 2026 Central American and Caribbean Games in Santo Domingo; and

WHEREAS, Yariel Soto Torrado is deserving of this prestigious title, a tribute to the remarkable athletic gifts and indomitable spirit that he so amply displayed at this year's Central American and Caribbean Games, where he placed first in the decathlon with a total of 7,884 points. A tenacious competitor, he can always be counted on to shine, and understandably, the strength, agility, stamina, and skill that he has exhibited have earned him many admirers; and

WHEREAS, Yariel Soto Torrado's accomplishments and leadership are a justifiable source of pride and an excellent reflection not only on him but also on his family, his coaches, and his hometown, and he has gained the respect of even his most determined rivals. Clearly, his enthusiastic involvement in athletics has enabled him to learn much about dedication, self-discipline, and leadership, and in the process, his exceptional prowess has set a fine example that is sure to inspire future generations; and

WHEREAS, The decathlon is a demanding event that requires exceptional power, endurance, and natural ability, and Yariel Soto Torrado is to be commended for his exemplary performance at this year's Central American and Caribbean Games. Throughout extensive practice and preparation, he has developed into a dynamic competitor, and it is our hope that he will take the lessons learned from these triumphs and apply them well as he pursues his goals in the years ahead; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, pay tribute to Yariel Soto Torrado on his tremendous athletic achievement and extend best wishes for the future; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Yariel Soto Torrado.
