sr477_01_AS As Adopted by the Senate 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 477 2025-2026 Senators Weinstein, Roegner A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Hudson High School girls soccer team as the 2026 Division II State Champion. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate the Hudson High School girls soccer team on winning the 2026 Division II State Championship; and WHEREAS, A remarkable group of young athletes, the members of the Hudson High School girls soccer team have combined talent and spirit with the highest ideals of good sportsmanship to establish themselves as truly exceptional competitors. With admirable dedication, the Explorers have worked diligently toward attaining ultimate proficiency in soccer, and as a result, they defeated a tough Bishop Watterson team by the score of 2-0 to win this year's Division II State Championship, their second such title in program history; and WHEREAS, The Explorers' achievements and leadership are a justifiable source of pride and an excellent reflection on the players, their families, their school, their community, and their coach, Josh Huettich. In leading his team to victory, Coach Huettich has not only helped the athletes to develop their talents to the fullest but also instilled in them a valuable sense of determination, perseverance, and camaraderie; and WHEREAS, With athletic competition playing such an important role in the total education of our young people, we are extremely pleased to pay tribute to these exemplary athletes and their outstanding coach for a job well done. The maturity and experience the team members have gained through their participation in sports will greatly aid them in meeting the challenges of the future; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend the Hudson High School girls soccer team on its tremendous season and salute the members and coach as some of Ohio's finest citizens; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Hudson High School girls soccer team.
