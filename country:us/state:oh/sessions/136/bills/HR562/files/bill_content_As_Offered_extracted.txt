hr562_00_IN As Offered 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 562 2025-2026 Representative Mathews, A. A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Mason High School girls tennis team on winning the 2026 OTCA Division I State Championship. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to commend the Mason High School girls tennis team as the 2026 Ohio Tennis Coaches Association Division I State Champion; and WHEREAS, The Mason High School girls tennis team has proven to be a talented and superbly conditioned squad and has gained the respect of fans and opponents alike for the spirited and highly motivated performance of its members. Throughout the season, the athletes worked diligently to improve their skills on the court, and as a result, they accomplished their goals, bringing home this year's state championship, their tenth consecutive title; and WHEREAS, Known as a driven and determined team, the members of the Mason High School girls tennis team have developed into a remarkably efficient group of athletes whose skill is equaled only by their exemplary display of teamwork. They are a credit not only to their families and their school but also to the superb leadership of their coach, Mike Reid; and WHEREAS, The road to victory is a difficult one, requiring ability, endurance, and a positive attitude on the part of players and coaches alike. The members of the Mason High School girls tennis team have exemplified all of the characteristics that are essential for success both in and out of competition, including courage, discipline, integrity, and good sportsmanship, and their efforts have earned them the admiration of all those who have witnessed these outstanding athletes in action; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, salute the Mason High School girls tennis team on its 2026 OTCA Division I State Championship and extend best wishes for the years to come; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Mason High School girls tennis team.
