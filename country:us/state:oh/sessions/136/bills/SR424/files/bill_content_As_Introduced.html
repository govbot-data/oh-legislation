A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring The Wooster Brush Company on its One Hundred Seventy-fifth Anniversary.

WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to commend The Wooster Brush Company as it celebrates the One Hundred Seventy-fifth Anniversary of its founding; and

WHEREAS, Since its inception, The Wooster Brush Company has experienced tremendous prosperity thanks to the valuable services it has provided to its customers. Over the course of the past one hundred seventy-five years, its exemplary commitment to the highest industry standards has never faltered, and its dedication to the best interests of its clients has been evident in all its transactions; and

WHEREAS, Ohio's economic well-being depends on forward-thinking and innovative entrepreneurs to provide goods and services to the citizens of our state. On the celebration of this event, we are proud to note that it is through the diligent efforts of individuals such as all those associated with The Wooster Brush Company, who are willing to invest in the future of their community, that the State of Ohio remains a pleasant place in which to live; and

WHEREAS, The noteworthy success of The Wooster Brush Company is attributable largely to the loyal service of its qualified and committed staff, as well as to the innovation and foresight of its management. Their combined efforts have earned the venture a well-deserved reputation for excellence and have distinguished the organization as a worthy enterprise that has contributed greatly to the area's financial stability; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, pay tribute to The Wooster Brush Company on its One Hundred Seventy-fifth Anniversary and salute it as one of Ohio's finest businesses; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to The Wooster Brush Company.
