A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring the Dahm Brothers Company, Inc., on its One Hundredth Anniversary.

WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to extend special recognition to the Dahm Brothers Company, Inc., on the memorable occasion of its One Hundredth Anniversary; and

WHEREAS, The Dahm Brothers Company is, indeed, deserving of high praise, for throughout a century of operation, this fine enterprise has attained a noteworthy record of accomplishment. Established in 1926, this full-service, family-owned residential roofing contractor has earned the gratitude and appreciation of many satisfied customers, and its success is a justifiable source of pride and an outstanding reflection not only on the business itself but also on its owners, Chris and Steve Dahm, on its hard-working employees, and on the community; and

WHEREAS, Since its founding, the Dahm Brothers Company has enhanced the quality of life within the surrounding area, and it has continually demonstrated its exemplary commitment to the highest industry standards, quality workmanship, and competitive pricing. We have no doubt that as this worthy enterprise maintains its dedication to service and achievement, it will continue to grow and prosper; and

WHEREAS, Over the years, the Dahm Brothers Company has epitomized America's entrepreneurial spirit, and it is through the contributions of people such as all those who direct and sustain it that our state has gained a reputation as a friendly, productive, and pleasant place in which to live. We are certain that in years to come, the members of the Dahm family, as well as the dedicated individuals that they employ, will uphold the tradition of excellence that has long been the hallmark of the business; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend the Dahm Brothers Company, Inc., on its Centennial and extend best wishes for continued success; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Dahm Brothers Company, Inc.
