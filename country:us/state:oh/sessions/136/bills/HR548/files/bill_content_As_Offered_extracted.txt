hr548_00_IN As Offered 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 548 2025-2026 Representative Brennan A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Parma City School District on its Bicentennial. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to pay tribute to the Parma City School District on the celebration of its Two Hundredth Anniversary; and WHEREAS, This is an important occasion, and congratulations are certainly in order. Established on May 13, 1826, the Parma City School District has experienced tremendous change over the past two centuries, and it has attained a tremendous record of service to the Parma, Parma Heights, and Seven Hills communities. It has truly earned the esteem of the many students who have passed through its doors; and WHEREAS, Schools are avenues that inspire a stronger society, and the Parma City School District has been steadfast in empowering each student for success through scholastic advancement, leadership, and preparedness. Clearly, this institution has grown thanks to the support of the community as well as to the values that have been introduced in the classroom, including respect, cooperation, and resolve; and WHEREAS, We look with optimism to a future that continues to thrive as a result of the diverse academic journey promoted by the Parma City School District, along with its mission to inspire, excite, and educate. Indeed, this educational resource is a treasure to area residents and an exceedingly well-regarded part of Ohio's public school systems; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend the Parma City School District on its Two Hundredth Anniversary and salute it as a fine Ohio educational institution; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Parma City School District.
