hr574_00_IN As Offered 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 574 2025-2026 Representative Russo A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team on winning the 2026 State Championship crown. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to salute the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team as the 2026 State Champion; and WHEREAS, Establishing a reputation for its skill and tenacity, the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team is an impressive group of athletes whose talent and teamwork are unmatched. At the 2026 State Championship, the Golden Bears secured the title for a second consecutive year, defeating Napoleon High School 9-5, and this victory will be celebrated for many years to come; and WHEREAS, The members of the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team rose to the occasion and proved themselves to be a dynamic force in high school sports. Truly, these outstanding athletes can be proud of the accomplishments that were hard-won and deserved throughout the 2026 season; and WHEREAS, By remaining faithful to the highest standards of sportsmanship and teamwork, the members of the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team primed themselves to win this year's State Championship title and reach their full potential. Indeed, the Golden Bears have demonstrated remarkable energy, enthusiasm, and dedication in all of their endeavors; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, recognize the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team on its success and honor the athletes and coaches for their tremendous prowess; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Upper Arlington High School girls water polo team.
