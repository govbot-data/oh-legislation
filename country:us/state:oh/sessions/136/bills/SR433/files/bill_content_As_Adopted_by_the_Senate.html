A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring the Toledo Fence Supply Company on its Centennial.

WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate the Toledo Fence & Supply Company on the auspicious occasion of its One Hundredth Anniversary; and

WHEREAS, Recognition of this prestigious milestone is a fitting tribute to the Toledo Fence & Supply Company, for throughout the past one hundred years, it has attained a remarkable record of service. Founded by Fred Arheit in 1926 as the Toledo Fence and Post Company, this family-owned enterprise fabricates, sells, installs, and repairs all types of fencing for residential, commercial, and industrial clients throughout Northwest Ohio and Southeast Michigan; and

WHEREAS, The success of the Toledo Fence & Supply Company is a justifiable source of pride and an outstanding reflection not only on the business itself but also on its astute management and hard‑working employees. Due to their vision and initiative, this respected institution has earned the gratitude and appreciation of many satisfied customers, and it is truly deserving of high praise; and

WHEREAS, Over the years, the Toledo Fence & Supply Company has enhanced the quality of life within the surrounding area. We are certain that as this noteworthy establishment maintains its unfaltering dedication to service and achievement, it will continue to prosper and will uphold the tradition of excellence that has long been its hallmark; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend the Toledo Fence & Supply Company on its One Hundredth Anniversary and salute it as one of Ohio's finest businesses; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Toledo Fence & Supply Company.
