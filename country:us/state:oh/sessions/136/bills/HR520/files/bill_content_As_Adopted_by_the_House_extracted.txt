hr520_01_AH As Adopted by the House 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 520 2025-2026 Representative Miller, K. A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Kendall Wild as the 2026 National Miss Amazing Teen Queen. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate Kendall Wild on being named the 2026 National Miss Amazing Teen Queen; and WHEREAS, Truly, Kendall Wild has brought exceptional ability to all of her endeavors, and as the 2026 National Miss Amazing Teen Queen, she has been entrusted to represent young women throughout the country. She is richly deserving of this title, which she earned through her community service and disability advocacy; and WHEREAS, Kendall Wild's distinctive qualities and engaging personality are invaluable to her reign as the 2026 National Miss Amazing Teen Queen. She has brought enthusiasm, confidence, and intention to promoting the causes she believes in, and her record of accomplishment promises to be not only her legacy but also a lasting example for Ohioans of all ages to emulate; and WHEREAS, Kendall Wild can be proud of attaining this high honor, for in channeling her many strengths, she has established a foundation for excellence rooted in visionary efforts, talent, intellect, and resolve. We are certain that in being named the 2026 National Miss Amazing Teen Queen, she will embody the principles of this distinguished role and accept each challenge as an opportunity for growth and achievement; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, celebrate Kendall Wild as the 2026 National Miss Amazing Teen Queen and salute her as one of our brightest citizens; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Kendall Wild.
