sr457_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 457 2025-2026 Senator Brenner A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Jodie Han on winning the 2026 OHSAA Division I State Championship in girls golf. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to applaud Jodie Han as the winner of the 2026 Ohio High School Athletic Association Division I State Championship in girls golf; and WHEREAS, A junior member of the Olentangy High School girls golf team, Jodie Han is deserving of acknowledgement for her exemplary performance at the OHSAA Division I State Tournament, where she secured a first-place finish with an impressive two-day record score of 129. Her athletic prowess as an outstanding competitor is reflected in this state crown, and her success stands as a hallmark for others; and WHEREAS, Through her participation in golf, Jodie Han has furthered on her growth and accomplishments by preparing diligently, and she has set an example worthy of emulation. Indeed, by implementing her remarkable discipline, perseverance, and hard work at the state level, she has inspired many to challenge themselves to strive for excellence; and WHEREAS, In a society that encourages individuals to reflect on the merits of advancing one's endeavors, athletic competition has become a valuable outlet for success. We are delighted to congratulate such a fine golfer as Jodie Han and look with optimism as she continues her development in the sport; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, extend special recognition to Jodie Han for her achievement in girls golf at the 2026 OHSAA Division I State Tournament and salute her as a fine Ohioan; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Jodie Han.
