Representative Moore


A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring the St. Clairsville High School Band Red Devil Indoor Percussion as a 2026 Ohio Indoor Performance Association Champion.

WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to commend the St. Clairsville High School Band Red Devil Indoor Percussion on winning the 2026 Ohio Indoor Performance Association Championship in the percussion regional A class; and

WHEREAS, A talented group of musicians, the St. Clairsville High School Band Red Devil Indoor Percussion has certainly set an example worthy of emulation. Under the able direction of its conductor, the group placed first at this year's Ohio Indoor Performance Association Championships in the percussion regional A class with its show, "Mad Doctor." Indeed, the band has earned a level of respect and recognition of which its members, both past and present, can be proud; and

WHEREAS, The members of the St. Clairsville High School Band Red Devil Indoor Percussion have achieved the balance, blend, and harmony that are the hallmarks of a superior ensemble. Its directors have not only encouraged the musicians to develop exceptional talent but have also reinforced the value of appreciating music and the positive role it plays in society; and

WHEREAS, Music is an integral component of our heritage, helping people to communicate across national, cultural, and social barriers, as well as uniting performers and audiences in a meaningful, shared experience. The members of the St. Clairsville High School Band Red Devil Indoor Percussion have imparted the richness and beauty of music to citizens throughout the area, and they are deserving of applause; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, congratulate the St. Clairsville High School Band Red Devil Indoor Percussion on its exemplary achievement at the 2026 OIPA Championships and salute all those associated with the ensemble as fine Ohioans; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the St. Clairsville High School Band Red Devil Indoor Percussion.
