Senator DeMora


A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring Ben Davino on winning a 2026 U23 World Championship.

WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to recognize Ben Davino on securing first place in the 61-kilogram freestyle category at the 2026 U23 World Championships in Las Vegas; and

WHEREAS, Displaying exceptional form, ability, leadership, and dedication during the season, Ben Davino has certainly distinguished himself as one of the most talented wrestlers in the nation. After finishing as the NCAA Division I runner up in the 133-pound weight class, he went on to place first in the 61-kilogram freestyle category at 2026 U23 World Championships, and he is deserving of high praise; and

WHEREAS, Throughout intense training and preparation, Ben Davino has consistently demonstrated the talent, integrity, and motivation that are the hallmarks of a true champion. To his credit, he could always be counted on not only to shine individually but also to contribute significantly to his teammates' collective efforts; and

WHEREAS, The first Ohio State University athlete to win a U23 championship, Ben Davino is truly worthy of acknowledgement for the formidable athletic gifts and the competitive spirit that he has so amply revealed throughout this past season. He has inspired his peers, thrilled his fans, gained the respect of his opponents, and made his coaches and family very proud; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, honor Ben Davino on his impressive wrestling accomplishments and wish him a bright, rewarding future; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Ben Davino.
