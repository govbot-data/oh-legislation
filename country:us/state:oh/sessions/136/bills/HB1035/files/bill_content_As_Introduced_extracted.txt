hb1035_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session	H. B. No. 1035 2025-2026 Representatives Richardson, Miller, K. To make an appropriation supporting medical residents placed in family medicine or psychiatry slots. BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF OHIO: Section 1. All items in this act are hereby appropriated as designated out of any moneys in the state treasury to the credit of the designated fund. For all operating appropriations made in this act, those in the first column are for fiscal year 2026 and those in the second column are for fiscal year 2027. The operating appropriations made in this act are in addition to any other operating appropriations made for these fiscal years. Section 2. 1 2 3 4 5 A DOH DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH B General Revenue Fund C GRF 440485 Health Program Support $0 $5,000,000 D General Revenue Fund Total $0 $5,000,000 E TOTAL ALL BUDGET FUND GROUPS $0 $5,000,000 HEALTH PROGRAM SUPPORT The foregoing appropriation item 440485, Health Program Support, shall be distributed to hospitals and used to support graduate medical education residency slots for residents placed in family medicine or psychiatry fields. The Department shall establish requirements regarding the distribution of funds, including the requirement that funds are used to support residents placed in family medicine or psychiatry slots. Section 3. Within the limits set forth in this act, the Director of Budget and Management shall establish accounts indicating the source and amount of funds for each appropriation made in this act, and shall determine the manner in which appropriation accounts shall be maintained. Expenditures from operating appropriations contained in this act shall be accounted for as though made in, and are subject to all applicable provisions of, H.B. 96 of the 136th General Assembly.
