hb1015_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session	H. B. No. 1015 2025-2026 Representative King To enact section 5534.632 of the Revised Code to designate a portion of the interchange of U.S. Route 33 and State Route 66 in Auglaize County as the "Sgt. Eli Holsinger Memorial Interchange." BE IT ENACTED BY THE GENERAL ASSEMBLY OF THE STATE OF OHIO: Section 1. That section 5534.632 of the Revised Code be enacted to read as follows: Sec. 5534.632. In addition to any other name prescribed in the Revised Code or otherwise, the interchange of United States route number thirty-three and state route number sixty-six, in Auglaize county, shall be known as the "Sgt. Eli Holsinger Memorial Interchange." The director of transportation may erect suitable markers at the interchange indicating its name.
