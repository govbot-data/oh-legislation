A R E S O L U T I O N

Honoring the Tuscarawas County Public Library System on its One Hundred Twenty-fifth Anniversary.

WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to pay tribute to the Tuscarawas County Public Library System as it commemorates its One Hundred Twenty-fifth Anniversary; and

WHEREAS, This is, indeed, an important occasion, and congratulations are certainly in order. Established in 1901, the Tuscarawas County Public Library System has experienced tremendous growth and prosperity over the past one hundred twenty-five years, and it has tirelessly served as a place for inspiration and discovery for innumerable people; and

WHEREAS, The Tuscarawas County Public Library System has become one of the community's most valuable public resources by offering numerous collections, local events, and countless important services. Thanks to the generous efforts of a host of area citizens, it remains an outstanding institution for its patrons as well as an exciting hub of language and learning that connects people through a variety of programming; and

WHEREAS, Those who have nurtured, directed, and sustained the Tuscarawas County Public Library System merit praise for their remarkable initiative, diligence, and resolve. With a wealth of vision and vitality, they have shown how very much can be accomplished by a group of conscientious, industrious people, and they can be proud that their unflagging commitment to literacy, education, and volunteer service has made it possible for the library system to flourish; therefore be it

RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, applaud the Tuscarawas County Public Library System on its One Hundred Twenty-fifth Anniversary and look with optimism to a bright future; and be it further

RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to the Tuscarawas County Public Library System.
