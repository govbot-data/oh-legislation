hr581_01_AH As Adopted by the House 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 581 2025-2026 Representative Hoops A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Gwen Huddle as a 2026 Division III State Cross Country Champion. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to extend special recognition to Gwen Huddle on placing first at the 2026 Division III State Cross Country Championship; and WHEREAS, Gwen Huddle has clearly demonstrated her talent and perseverance, as well as her mental and physical fortitude, and she is deserving of acknowledgement. A junior member of the Napoleon High School girls cross country team, she has exemplified all of the characteristics that are essential for success both in and out of the sports world, including courage, determination, and an innate sense of the principles of good sportsmanship, and consequently, she placed first at the 2026 Division III State Championship meet with a time of 17:35.7; and WHEREAS, Gwen Huddle has conscientiously applied herself to the quest for excellence as an athlete, training and practicing diligently in order to ensure peak conditioning. Time and again, she has risen to the occasion, maintaining her morale and composure, and she has gained the appreciation of all those who have followed her this season; and WHEREAS, Cross country is an event that requires exceptional stamina and natural ability, and Gwen Huddle is to be commended for her commitment to this demanding sport. In attaining her athletic goals, she has consistently exhibited the skill and integrity that are the hallmarks of a true champion; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, congratulate Gwen Huddle as the 2026 Division III State Cross Country Champion and offer best wishes for the future; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Gwen Huddle.
