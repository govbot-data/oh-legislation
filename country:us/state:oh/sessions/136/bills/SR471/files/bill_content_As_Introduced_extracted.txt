sr471_00_IN As Introduced 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 471 2025-2026 Senator Cutrona A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Stambaugh Auditorium on its Centennial. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to congratulate Stambaugh Auditorium on the auspicious occasion of its One Hundredth Anniversary, December 5, 2026; and WHEREAS, Recognition of this prestigious milestone is a fitting tribute to Stambaugh Auditorium, for throughout the past century, this fine event venue has attained a remarkable record of service to the Youngstown area. Established in 1926 in honor of the late Henry H. Stambaugh, this concert hall and performance space has hosted a wide variety of events and performances over the years, and despite undergoing both renovations and reconstructions, it has retained a unique charm and character that continues to endear it to generations of patrons; and WHEREAS, The success of Stambaugh Auditorium is a justifiable source of pride and an outstanding reflection not only on the venue itself but also on its astute leadership and hard‑working employees. This respected endeavor has earned the gratitude and appreciation of many satisfied patrons due to the vision and initiative of its staff, and it is truly deserving of high praise; and WHEREAS, Over the years, Stambaugh Auditorium has enhanced the quality of life within the surrounding area. We are certain that as this worthy establishment maintains its unwavering dedication to service, achievement, and the advancement of the arts, it will continue to prosper and will uphold the tradition of excellence that has long been its hallmark; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, commend Stambaugh Auditorium on its One Hundredth Anniversary and extend best wishes for the future; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Stambaugh Auditorium.
