hr537_00_IN As Offered 136th General Assembly Regular Session H. R. No. 537 2025-2026 Representatives Young, White, A. A R E S O L U T I O N Honoring Shook Construction on its One Hundredth Anniversary. WHEREAS, The members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to commend Shook Construction on the auspicious occasion of its One Hundredth Anniversary; and WHEREAS, Recognition of this prestigious milestone is, indeed, a fitting tribute to Shook Construction, for throughout the past century, this fine business has attained a remarkable record of service. Since its founding in Dayton by Charles H. Shook in 1926, the company has continually demonstrated its exemplary commitment to the highest industry standards, customer satisfaction, and competitive pricing, and its dedication to the best interests of its clientele has been evident in all its transactions; and WHEREAS, The success of Shook Construction is a justifiable source of pride and an outstanding reflection not only on the business itself but also on its astute management and hard-working employees. Their vision and initiative have earned this respected institution the gratitude and appreciation of many satisfied customers, and they are truly deserving of high praise; and WHEREAS, Over the years, Shook Construction has enhanced the quality of life within the surrounding community and contributed greatly to the area's economic well-being. We are certain that as this worthy enterprise maintains its dedication to service and achievement, it will continue to prosper and will uphold the tradition of excellence that has long been its hallmark; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the House of Representatives of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, congratulate Shook Construction on its Centennial and extend best wishes for the future; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the House of Representatives transmit a duly authenticated copy of this Resolution to Shook Construction.
