sr476_01_AS As Adopted by the Senate 136th General Assembly Regular Session S. R. No. 476 2025-2026 Senators Weinstein, Johnson A R E S O L U T I O N Recognizing Military Family Appreciation Month in Ohio, November 2026. WHEREAS, The members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio are pleased to observe November 2026 as Military Family Appreciation Month in Ohio; and WHEREAS, President Donald Trump has designated November 2026 as Military Family Appreciation Month in an effort to recognize the sacrifices and contributions of military families and veterans. At the heart of our national defense are military families, whose strength at home allows our service members to protect the country. Driven by love for their families and belief in America, military family members navigate the hardships of frequent moves, long deployments, and time spent apart with incredible grace and resilience, and they are worthy of acknowledgement for their strength and perseverance. In peace or conflict, they are patriots who confront challenges with dignity and resolve; and WHEREAS, Military families exemplify the finest qualities of citizenship through their commitment to community, service, and personal responsibility. Whether supporting military installations, volunteering in local organizations, mentoring young people, or contributing to the economic vitality of their communities, they play a significant role in strengthening the social fabric of our state and nation; and WHEREAS, The heroic traditions of our country's armed forces are an important part of our American heritage. Generations of U.S. military personnel have provided a valuable service to our country and established a record of achievement that will long stand as a hallmark worthy of emulation. By devoting their time, energy, and abilities to protecting our nation, and by steadfastly supporting those efforts at home, our service members and their families have gained the respect and admiration of all those with whom they have served and the appreciation of countless Americans; therefore be it RESOLVED, That we, the members of the Senate of the 136th General Assembly of Ohio, in adopting this Resolution, recognize November 2026 as Military Family Appreciation Month in Ohio and applaud military families for their tenacity and courage; and be it further RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Senate transmit duly authenticated copies of this Resolution to the news media of Ohio.
